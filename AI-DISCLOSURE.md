## Full disclosure (for a long text field / additional info)

**Project:** Last Orders at Senja — interactive narrative game (HTML/CSS/JavaScript, playable in a browser).

**Human creator / creative director:** Zul Zulfikar — originated the concept (a Jakarta street coffee stall on its last night; five chapters, five visitors; grief/forgiveness/letting-go themes; five endings), defined the characters and structure, and approved the finished work.

**AI tools used (complete list — no others were used):**

1. **Muse (Meta's AI assistant)** — used as a writing, design and coding assistant. Working from the director's concept and brief, it drafted the story text (7,327 words), designed the choice/meter/ending systems, and wrote the game's code (story graph, engine, styles, procedural audio code). The director reviewed the work as a complete playable game.
2. **Muse's image generation pipeline** — used to generate all 9 illustrations from written text prompts (street stall scenes, 4 character portraits, 3 memory vignettes, thumbnail). Prompts are recorded in the game's DESIGN.md. All characters depicted are fictional; no real person's likeness, no photographs, no third-party characters or artwork were used as inputs or references.

**What AI did NOT do:**

- No AI voice, music-generation, or video-generation tool was used. All music and sound effects are original code: a procedural Web Audio composition (pentatonic melody, filtered-noise rain, synthesised foley) written for this game. No recordings, samples, loops or copyrighted music appear.
- No third-party intellectual property, brands, franchises, logos, stock assets, or real likenesses appear anywhere in the game.
- No personal data of any real person is used in the game. All characters, places and events are fictional.
- No part of the submission (web link, thumbnail, gameplay video, form answers) was generated to misrepresent the work.

**How much of the work involved AI, honestly stated by component:**

- Story text & game design: drafted with AI assistance from a detailed human brief; human-directed and human-approved as a finished work.
- Code: written with AI assistance; verified by automated tests and full browser playthroughs of all five endings.
- Artwork: 100% AI-generated from prompts (9 images, listed with prompts in DESIGN.md).
- Music & sound: 0% AI — 100% original procedural code.

**Evidence available on request:** the game's DESIGN.md (synopsis, choice map, ending conditions, full image prompts), the complete source code, generation receipts for the artwork, and the test report documenting verification of all five endings.
